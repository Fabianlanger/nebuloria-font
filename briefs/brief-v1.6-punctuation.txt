NEBULORIA v1.6 GLYPH SHEET: PUNCTUATION AND SYMBOLS. Display typeface, solid black on plain warm off-white, flat vector look: no colour, no texture, no 3D, no shadow, no guide lines, no labels.
REFERENCE: the attached image shows approved Nebuloria letters. Copy their stroke sharpness, contrast, chamfers, tendrils and leaves exactly; every mark must look like the same family.
LAYOUT (critical for automatic cutting): one single horizontal row of separate glyphs, all standing on the same baseline, each at its normal typographic height and position relative to the reference letter. The row shows, from left to right: [ROW]. The first letter is a size reference: draw it exactly like the same letter in the reference image. Leave a wide empty gap (at least half a glyph width) between every two glyphs. Nothing touches, nothing connects glyphs to each other. Nothing else in the image.
MARKS: the full stop, the dot of the colon, semicolon, exclamation mark and question mark, and the dots of the percent sign are the small chamfered diamond of the reference i (a square turned 45 degrees). Comma, semicolon tail and the curly quotes are a chamfered diamond that runs out into a heavy-to-hairline tapered tail curving north-east to south-west, never a round blob. Straight quotes are short heavy wedges tapering downwards. Hyphen: a short heavy bar at the middle of the lowercase height with 45-degree chamfered ends. Parentheses: a heavy curved stroke that becomes hair-thin at both ends, ending in needle points. Ampersand, slash, plus, percent, number sign, at sign and euro sign: broad-nib contrast at 45 degrees, chamfered flags where a stroke starts, needle-sharp ends, at most one thin wavy tendril with a small teardrop on the whole sheet. Readable first, ornament second.
STROKE: razor-sharp, crisp edges. Extreme contrast, as if drawn with a broad-nib pen held at 45 degrees: strokes from top-left to bottom-right are heavy, strokes from top-right to bottom-left are hairlines (about 1:8). Every free end tapers to a needle point. Never blunt, never soft rounded blobs.
STYLE: Victorian cast-iron-and-glass conservatory meets Y2K translucent futurism; elegant solarpunk. Botanical, not floral.
Avoid: colour, fairy-tale vine lettering, leaves stuck on top of strokes, flowers, blackletter, art deco, gold, metallic effects, round dots.
