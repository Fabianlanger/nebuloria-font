THE FAMILY'S STROKE TECHNIQUES (every redesigned letter must show them):
T1 Broad-nib contrast at 45 degrees: strokes moving from top-left to bottom-right are HEAVY, strokes moving from top-right to bottom-left are HAIRLINE thin. Extreme contrast.
T2 Crisp straight 45-degree chamfer cuts on outer corners and stroke ends (top-left corners of stems, corners of bowls), like the approved B D E P R and the octagonal O.
T3 Needle-sharp points: every free end tapers to a sharp point or runs out into a long hairline tail along the baseline. Never blunt, never rounded blobs.
T4 Capitals only: a thin wavy tendril runs alongside the main heavy stroke, starting at the chamfered top-left corner and ending in a small teardrop, exactly like the approved F and H.
T5 Asymmetric feet: serif feet are longer to the right and taper into hairline tails.
No sprouts, leaves or extra ornaments except the small leaf inside closed counters (as in the approved B, D, P, R).
