WIRE LIGATURES for the Nebuloria typeface. Solid black on plain warm off-white, flat vector look, no colour, no texture, no shadow.
LAYOUT (critical for automatic cutting): one single horizontal row of separate letter PAIRS on one shared baseline, all at the same size. Leave a very wide empty gap (at least one letter width) between pairs. Within a pair the two letters keep their normal spacing. Nothing else in the image.
THE LETTERS: copy each letter EXACTLY as drawn in REFERENCE LETTERS (same shapes, chamfers, tendrils, leaves, weight, size). Do not redesign the letters.
THE WIRE (the only new element): inside every pair, one thin round hanging liana connects the first letter to the second, exactly like the wires in REFERENCE LANGER v0.4 (L to a, n to g, e to r): it grows out of the first letter's top-right (a terminal, arm end or tendril), hangs down in a smooth round U or soft S-curve below the x-height, and grows up into the second letter's top-left. It is a smooth, flowing, round hairline of the same thickness as the tendrils, never straight, never angular, never a V or a spike. It may end in a tiny teardrop where it meets the second letter. It must not cover the letters or reduce their legibility.
