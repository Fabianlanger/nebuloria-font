NEBULORIA v1.0 GLYPH SHEET. Display typeface, solid black on plain warm off-white, flat vector look: no colour, no texture, no 3D, no shadow, no guide lines, no labels.
LAYOUT (critical for automatic cutting): one single horizontal row of separate letters, all on the same baseline, all at the same size. Leave a wide empty gap (at least half a letter width) between every two letters. No wires, tendrils or ligatures connecting one letter to another; each letter is a complete, isolated shape. Nothing else in the image.
STROKE SHARPNESS (most important, copy REFERENCE LANGER v0.2 exactly): razor-sharp, crisp edges. Every taper runs out into a needle-fine point or a hairline-thin tail along the baseline. Terminals and spurs end in sharp points, never soft rounded blobs. Chamfered corners are crisp straight 45-degree cuts. Extreme contrast between heavy stems and hair-thin strokes, like a sharp broad-nib pen at 45 degrees.
LOWERCASE STYLE: the lowercase of REFERENCE LANGER v0.2 (shapes may also follow REFERENCE LANGER v0.4 and the lowercase b, i, a, n of REFERENCE B): rounded bowls with a few crisp 45-degree chamfered outer corners, heavy straight stems, hairline tails along the baseline, a thin wavy hairline tendril ending in a small teardrop on some letters (only inside the letter's own space), a small sharp-pointed leaf inside the counter of letters with a closed bowl (a, b, d, g, o, p, q). The i and j have a small chamfered diamond dot.
CAPITAL STYLE: the capital F of REFERENCE A (Fabian v0.5 A): heavy stems with crisp 45-degree chamfered top-left corners, a thin wavy tendril running alongside the main stem ending in a teardrop, and a sharp pointed leaf-shaped flag at the left end of the top horizontal arm. The capital F's foot serif must be SHORT on the right side so the F can never be mistaken for an E. Capitals with arms (E, F, L, T, Z) share that pointed leaf flag. The L follows the L of REFERENCE LANGER v0.2.
Avoid: soft or blunt rounded ends, blurry edges, colour, fairy-tale vine lettering, leaves on top of strokes, art deco, gold, connecting strokes between letters.
