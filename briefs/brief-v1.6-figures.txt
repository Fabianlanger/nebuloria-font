NEBULORIA v1.6 GLYPH SHEET: FIGURES. Display typeface, solid black on plain warm off-white, flat vector look: no colour, no texture, no 3D, no shadow, no guide lines, no labels.
REFERENCE: the attached image shows approved Nebuloria letters. Copy their stroke sharpness, contrast, chamfers, tendrils and leaves exactly; the figures must look like the same family.
LAYOUT (critical for automatic cutting): one single horizontal row of separate glyphs, all standing on the same baseline. The row shows, from left to right: [ROW]. The capital H is a size reference: draw it exactly like the reference H. All figures are lining figures: exactly as tall as the capital H, sitting on the same baseline. Leave a wide empty gap (at least half a glyph width) between every two glyphs. Nothing touches, nothing connects glyphs to each other. Nothing else in the image.
STROKE: razor-sharp, crisp edges. Extreme contrast, as if drawn with a broad-nib pen held at 45 degrees: strokes from top-left to bottom-right are heavy, strokes from top-right to bottom-left are hairlines (about 1:8). Every free end tapers to a needle point or runs out into a hairline tail along the baseline. Never blunt, never soft rounded blobs.
CONSTRUCTION: a curve first runs straight, then turns; the stroke becomes hair-thin where it turns. Corners and stroke ends are cut at crisp 45-degree chamfers; bowls where the weight flows stay round. Diagonals and curls only run north-east to south-west. Serif feet are asymmetric, longer to the right.
FIGURES: readable first, ornament second. The 1 has a chamfered flag at the top-left and an asymmetric foot. A closed counter (0, 6, 8, 9, and the 4 if closed) may hold one small sharp-pointed leaf growing from the inside of the stroke. At most one thin wavy tendril with a small teardrop per figure, and only on figures with a straight heavy stem (1, 4, 7). The 0 is an upright oval, clearly different from the letter O by being narrower. The 7 has a straight diagonal, not a curl.
STYLE: Victorian cast-iron-and-glass conservatory meets Y2K translucent futurism; elegant solarpunk. Botanical, not floral.
Avoid: colour, fairy-tale vine lettering, leaves stuck on top of strokes, flowers, blackletter, art deco, gold, metallic effects, old-style figures, figures of different heights.
FAMILY TECHNIQUES (a figure that looks like a plain serif figure is rejected): apply all family techniques of the reference letters C, G, b and d: broad-nib contrast at 45 degrees, a crisp chamfered flag at the top-left entry of every figure, needle-sharp ends, asymmetric tapering feet, and the outer corners of bowls cut at crisp 45-degree chamfers like the reference b and d (an octagonal rhythm), while the inside of the bowl flows. The heavy weight of the figures equals the heavy stems of the reference letters.
